# Brief structure

**Title:** "Housing in <Place>: <the one-line takeaway>", e.g. "Housing in South Bend: owners are driving growth while half of renters are stretched".

**Date line:** date, data vintages used, and the companion dashboard link if any.

## 1. Recommendations (3–5)
Numbered, each 2–4 sentences:
- **What to do** (a verb and an object: "Prioritize preservation of the 678 HUD-assisted units whose contracts expire by 2030").
- **Why**, with the evidence number(s) and comparison.
- **Lever and owner** (who can act: city, county, PHA, state HFA, employers, philanthropy).

Useful patterns from the data:
- High renter cost burden + renter income far below owner income → deeper affordability (vouchers, LIHTC 30% AMI units, rental assistance).
- Housing wage above the metro median wage → wage/housing-cost gap; employer-assisted housing, workforce pipeline.
- Input prices far above construction wages and CPI, low building-construction LQ → construction cost and capacity; infill incentives, pre-approved plans, fee deferrals, contractor pipeline.
- Expiring project-based contracts, Troubled/Substandard PHAs → preservation and PHA capacity.
- High "other vacant" share and old stock → vacancy-to-occupancy and rehab programs.
- Homeownership gaps by race → down-payment assistance, heirs'-property and title clearing, counseling.

## 2. What the data shows
Three short subsections: **Demand**, **Cost to build**, **Assisted housing**. Give 3–4 sentences or a 3–5 row table each, and compare with the region, state and U.S.

## 3. Regional context
One paragraph or a compact table: the focus against the ring counties on renter share, median rent, rent burden and assisted units per 100 renters.

## 4. Data notes
Bullets: sources and vintages, ACS margins of error for city figures, voucher floors, national PPI, regional CPI, and what is not covered (permits, evictions, CHAS).

Length: one page ≈ 450–550 words (recommendations + 2 short sections + notes). Two to three pages allows tables.
