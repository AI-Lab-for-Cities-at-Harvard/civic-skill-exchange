# Immunization reminder letter

Use this when a nurse or clerk needs a reminder letter for a family whose child is due or overdue for a routine vaccine.

## What you need

- The child's first name and the guardian's name.
- The vaccines that are due, and the date each became due.
- The clinic's address, hours, and phone number, and whether walk-ins are accepted.
- The language the family prefers, if not English.

## Steps

1. Open with the child's name and say plainly which vaccines are due.
2. Say why it matters in one sentence, without alarm.
3. Give the clinic's address, hours and phone number, and say whether an appointment is needed.
4. Say the shots are free or low-cost if the clinic offers that, and what to bring (the child's vaccine record, insurance card if any).
5. Close with a phone number for questions.

Keep it under 200 words, at about a 6th-grade reading level. Do not include any medical history beyond the due vaccines. Mark anything you were not given as [TO FILL] rather than guessing.
