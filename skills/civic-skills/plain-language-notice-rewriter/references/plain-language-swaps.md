## Structural patterns

**Passive to active.** "Your application was denied" → "We denied your
application." Name who acted. People need to know who to call.

**Nominalization to verb.** "Submission of documentation is required by March 1"
→ "Send us your documents by March 1."

**Conditional stacks to lists.** A sentence with three "if" clauses becomes three
bullets. The reader only needs the one that applies to them.

**Exceptions after rules.** State the rule, then the exception. Never open with
"Except as provided in subsection (c)".

## What not to simplify

- Statutory citations — keep them, but move them out of the sentence a person must
  act on
- Legally operative terms your statute defines — define them inline instead
- Dollar amounts, dates, and deadlines — never round or approximate
- The name of a form or program, exactly as it appears on the form itself
