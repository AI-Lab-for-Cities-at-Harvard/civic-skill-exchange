# Required elements by notice type

A generic starting checklist. **Replace this with your own.** Your statutes, your
appeal windows, your contact conventions — a generic list is a starting point, not
a compliance document.

Nothing here is legal advice. Confirm your requirements with counsel.

## Adverse determination (denial, reduction, termination)

- [ ] What was decided, in the first sentence
- [ ] The legal or regulatory basis
- [ ] The specific facts the decision rested on
- [ ] The effective date
- [ ] The right to appeal
- [ ] The appeal deadline, as a calendar date
- [ ] How to appeal, including where to get the form
- [ ] Whether benefits continue during appeal, and what the reader must do to
      continue them
- [ ] Where to get help, including free legal services if applicable
- [ ] Language access statement
- [ ] Nondiscrimination statement
- [ ] A contact for questions

## Request for information

- [ ] What is needed, itemized
- [ ] Why it is needed
- [ ] The deadline, as a calendar date
- [ ] How to submit — every accepted channel
- [ ] What happens if it is not received
- [ ] Who to contact for an extension
- [ ] Language access statement

## Approval or award

- [ ] What was approved
- [ ] The amount or scope
- [ ] The effective and end dates
- [ ] What the reader must do next, if anything
- [ ] Reporting obligations and their deadlines
- [ ] What to do if circumstances change
- [ ] A contact for questions

## Deadline or expiration

- [ ] What expires
- [ ] The date, as a calendar date
- [ ] What happens on that date
- [ ] How to renew or extend, and by when
- [ ] What it costs
- [ ] A contact for questions

---

## Two elements people drop most often

**Calendar dates, not relative ones.** "Within 30 days of the date of this notice"
requires the reader to know when the notice was dated, when they received it, and
which of those starts the clock. Print the date.

**Where to get the form.** Naming a form without saying where to get it is one of
the most common failures in government correspondence. Give a URL, a phone number,
and a physical location.
