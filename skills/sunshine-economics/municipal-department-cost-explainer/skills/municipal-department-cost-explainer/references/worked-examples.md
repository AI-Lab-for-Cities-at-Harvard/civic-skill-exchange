# Worked examples: fixed public-budget snapshots
These are illustrations, not current budget advice or cross-city comparisons.
Each uses USD whole dollars and the named version only. AI-assisted source and
arithmetic checks do not constitute municipal finance practitioner review.

## Escondido: allocations make a headline much smaller
Source E1: City of Escondido, FY2026/27 Final Operating Budget, FY2026/27 BUDGET
column; printed page and one-based PDF page numbers coincide.
https://www.escondido.gov/DocumentCenter/View/9773/FY2027-Operating-Budget-Final-PDF

| Reported item | Dollars | Page |
|---|---:|---|
| Fund 1001 personnel | 2,778,270 | 52 |
| Maintenance/operations | 144,500 | 53 |
| Internal services | 277,770 | 54 |
| Gross subtotal | 3,200,540 | 54 |
| Allocated out | (3,008,510) | 54 |
| Net headline | 192,030 | 54 |
| Fund 1002 deputy salary | 102,940 | 181 |
| Fund 1002 assistant salary | 82,770 | 181 |

Calculation: 2,778,270 + 144,500 + 277,770 = 3,200,540;
3,200,540 - 3,008,510 = 192,030. Allocations distribute existing cost, not
additional expense. The two Measure I salaries add 185,710 outside Fund 1001;
3,386,250 is only a documented subtotal including those salaries. Pooled employee
overhead on page 182 is not department-specific. A complete all-fund cost cannot
be established from those lines; do not allocate pooled overhead by guesswork.
Ask Finance for department-specific Measure I benefits and other legal-cost scope.

## San Diego: components and adjustments are not additional totals
Source S1: City of San Diego FY2026 Adopted Budget, City Attorney chapter,
FY2026 Adopted column; printed page 17 = PDF page 6.
https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/fy26ab_v2cityattorney.pdf

| Reported item | Dollars |
|---|---:|
| Personnel | 89,215,833 |
| Nonpersonnel | 6,600,102 |
| Department expenditures | 95,815,935 |
| Department revenue | 9,350,965 |

89,215,833 + 6,600,102 = 95,815,935. The division table on the same page
repeats that total; it is a breakdown, not another expenditure. Significant
adjustments on printed pages 17-19 explain the adopted budget and must not be
added again. The difference between the reported departmental expenses and
revenue is 86,464,970, not a certified citywide taxpayer-cost figure. The chapter
alone does not establish all citywide litigation, settlement or capital-related
legal costs. Budgeted FTE are not actual headcount. Ask Finance for any separately
budgeted legal costs and confirm the boundary before producing an all-in figure.

## Boston: recommended budget and external funds
Source B1: Boston FY2026 Recommended Budget, Mayor's Office Cabinet, April 2025.
Printed pages 27, 29, 30, 31 and 35 = PDF pages 23, 25, 26, 27 and 31.
https://www.boston.gov/sites/default/files/file/2025/04/V2%2001-%2026%20A%20Mayors-Office-Cabinet.pdf

| Reported item | Dollars | Printed page |
|---|---:|---|
| Ordinary departmental personnel | 8,593,503 | 27, 29 |
| Nonpersonnel | 2,349,000 | 27 |
| Ordinary operating budget | 10,942,503 | 27, 29 |
| Separately shown external spending | 300,000 | 27, 31 |

8,593,503 + 2,349,000 = 10,942,503. Including the separate external-fund
spending yields 11,242,503 for these schedules only. The external fund concerns
third-party property-damage repairs (page 35); do not equate all its spending
with lawyer compensation. This is a recommended budget, not adopted or actual.
The personnel detail lists 8,828,603, 139,441 and (374,542), and reports
8,593,503; arithmetic on those components gives 8,593,502. Preserve the reported amount, flag the $1 discrepancy,
and request clarification rather than silently changing it. These schedules
cannot certify all-in economic cost.

## Fictional example: incomplete evidence
Synthetic facts: a department reports 1,000,000 gross, including an internal IT
charge of 50,000, then allocates 300,000 to Water and reports 700,000 net. Water
shows the matching 300,000. Another fund has a 120,000 legal contract with no
specified beneficiary or overlap; a transfer of 120,000 also appears.

Correct treatment: preserve 1,000,000 gross and 700,000 net. Do not add the Water
counterpart, remove the IT charge from departmental appropriation, or add the
transfer as cost. Keep the 120,000 contract unresolved until its scope and overlap
are documented. An incorporated salary reduction is not subtracted again.
Do not manufacture a citywide total or label a partial number a lower bound.
