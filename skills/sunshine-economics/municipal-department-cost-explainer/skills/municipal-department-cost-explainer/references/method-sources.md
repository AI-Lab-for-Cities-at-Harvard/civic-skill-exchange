# Method sources and provenance
Created by Jennifer Schoeneck, CEcD - Sunshine Economics, with AI assistance.
Initial release: 2026-10-22. These sources inform design, not endorsement.

- Government Finance Officers Association, Public Engagement in the Budget Process
  (board approval September 28, 2018): understandable information, shared facts,
  trade-offs and professional judgment.
  https://www.gfoa.org/materials/public-engagement-in-the-budget-process
- GFOA, Budget Monitoring: distinguish budget from actual results.
  https://www.gfoa.org/materials/budget-monitoring
- GFOA, Capital Budget Presentation: explain capital funding and operating implications.
  https://www.gfoa.org/materials/capital-budget-presentation
- Civic Skill Exchange contribution contract: portable instructions, explicit
  metadata, bounded permissions and accurate deployment claims.
  https://github.com/AI-Lab-for-Cities-at-Harvard/civic-skill-exchange/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

These controls are the author's proposed workflow, not an accounting standard,
causal research finding, GFOA certification or audit. Establish local presentation
from local budget notes. No production deployment or human practitioner sign-off
is asserted. No Harvard affiliation, partnership or endorsement is claimed.

Original prose and templates are MIT licensed. Third-party documents retain their
own terms; they are linked, not redistributed. Numerical examples cite municipal
publications. Source snapshots are examples, not current financial advice.
