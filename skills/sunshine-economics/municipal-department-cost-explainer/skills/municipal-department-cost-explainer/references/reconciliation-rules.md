# Reconciliation rules
Department appropriation, all-fund spending, consolidated city cost, net funding
need and full economic cost are distinct measures. Name the measure before totaling.

## Evidence ledger
Use stable item/source IDs. Columns: item; fund; year/status; basis/units; amount;
classification; relationship to headline; include/exclude/unresolved; reason;
printed page/PDF page/table/column citation.

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Components of a reported total | Use as checks/detail; never add again to total. |
| Another fund's departmental expense | Add only if distinct, same scope/year/basis, and not a transfer or duplicate. |
| Grant, fee, recovery, reimbursement | Funding, not expense; verify attribution before net calculation. |
| Transfer between funds | Financing; locate the actual expense it finances. |
| Internal-service/overhead charge | Retain in department appropriation; eliminate only for verified consolidation of both sides. |
| Budget change or amendment | Establish whether already included in the reported total. |
| Negative appropriation or savings | Preserve sign; do not subtract an incorporated reduction twice. |
| Net headline after allocations out | Reverse documented offsets to recover gross; recipient allocations are not extra costs on top of that gross. |
| Outside counsel, claims, settlements | Establish organizational boundary; a legal keyword is not enough. |
| Budgeted positions/FTE | Not actual employees or an additional dollar amount. |
| Capital/debt | Separate from operating unless explicitly within scope. |
| Missing/conflicting amounts | Unknown/unresolved; never estimate silently. |

## Arithmetic
Reported headline + proven non-overlapping additional expenses - verified
duplicates in the starting aggregate = documented expenditure for stated scope.
Show each adjustment, evidence and equation. Possible overlap is not an addition.
Do not manufacture a balancing item.

Show funding separately. Where supported, state the net measure's expenditure
base and eligible offsets. Do not subtract both a recovery and its matching
transfer. A fund label alone does not establish legal restrictions.

## Completeness
Reconciled within supplied schedules does not mean audited citywide full cost.
Identify missing central overhead, pension, insurance, capital, debt or outside
counsel schedules when relevant. Do not assume these are absent, incremental or
excluded from the existing totals. Keep years, versions and bases distinct.
Report rounding rather than inventing a balancing item. Do not infer efficiency
from spending per resident or per budgeted FTE.
